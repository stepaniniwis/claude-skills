### P1 — Core construct string
`("entrepreneurial identity" OR "founder identity") AND ("identity work" OR "role identity" OR "social identity")`, from 2009.
Top-10 hits included: Fauchart & Gruber 2011 (10.5465/amj.2009.0211) ✅ anchor; Leitch & Harrison 2016 (10.1080/08985626.2016.1155740) ✅ prior-review anchor; plus gender/embodiment identity-work stream (Swail & Marlow 2017; IJGE 2024) → confirms Block A recall and surfaces intersectional stream for the sample.

### P2 — Platform/gig string
`("gig economy" OR "platform work" OR "gig workers") AND ("identity work" OR "occupational identity") AND entrepreneur*`, from 2015.
Hit #1: Alacovska et al., Multimodal identity work (10.1177/00187267241304591) ✅ anchor. Remaining hits mostly generic gig-economy reviews (algorithmic management, precarity) — identity terms are load-bearing; without them the string drowns. Petriglieri et al. 2019 (ASQ) NOT in top 10 → backward snowballing mandatory for platform cell.

### P3 — Corporate/academic/hybrid string
`(intrapreneur* OR "corporate entrepreneur*" OR "hybrid entrepreneur*" OR "academic entrepreneur*") AND identit*`, from 2009.
Strong academic-entrepreneur coverage: "Becoming an academic entrepreneur" (10.1007/s11187-021-00585-3); "Nascent academic entrepreneurs and identity work" (10.1177/14657503211063896); collective entrepreneurial identity in corporate venturing (10.1108/ijebr-08-2019-0492). Candidate additions for Academic × Hybrid and Corporate × Hybrid cells.

### P4 — Transitional string
`("entrepreneurial exit" OR "ex-entrepreneur" OR "business failure") AND ("identity loss" OR "identity conflict" OR "identity transition")`, from 2010.
Hit #1: Nielsen & Gish, When old and new selves collide (10.1111/peps.12626) ✅ anchor. Also surfaced: passion & role identity management process model (10.1002/job.2605) — candidate for PERSISTENCE/ACTION cells.
