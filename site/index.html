02 — Themes

Three questions
that hold the work together.

i.

Trustworthy Technology

Technologies that are not only capable but verifiable, resilient and institutionally viable. Digital trust, AI, defence tech, deep tech, sovereignty.

ii.

Institutions in Transition

How public and private institutions can adopt new technology legitimately, legibly and effectively. Governance, civic participation, institutional trust.

iii.

Work, Identity and Entrepreneurship

How people build careers and identities under shifting technological and economic conditions. Hybrid entrepreneurship, future of work, life design.

Running through everything: How do we shape identity, technology and institutions — and how do they shape us?

07 — About

Three practices,
one line of thinking.

I am a researcher, strategist, writer and model. I have spent more than fifteen years moving between ventures, institutions and research — founding companies, building technology and innovation programmes, advising organisations, and studying how emerging technologies change trust, work and identity. My work connects three worlds that are usually kept apart — technological systems, institutional change and human experience. Image and cultural practice are part of the same enquiry, not a parallel life.

Builder

Lateration, Secublox, SpaceFounders and further entrepreneurial work in deep tech and innovation programmes.

Researcher

Universität der Bundeswehr, dissertation on hybrid entrepreneurship, verifiable civic participation, AI agents, defence research.

Writer, Curator & Image-maker

Studio Stepanini, Bibliothek der Gefühle, Let’s Dine, Le Salon, editorial and modelling work, and the Substack Proof & Possibility.
